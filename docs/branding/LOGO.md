# Logo do Wallpaper Engine Scene Exporter

Criada com GPT Image pela ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens.

- Original PNG com transparência: `src/WallpaperSceneExporter/Assets/brand-mark.png`.
- Ícone Windows: `src/WallpaperSceneExporter/Assets/app.ico`, com tamanhos de 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 e 256 pixels.
- Para refazer apenas a conversão de formato, execute `scripts/Convert-AppIcon.ps1` no Windows.
- Aplicação: cabeçalho, ícone do executável, janelas e barra de tarefas.

O símbolo combina quadros sobrepostos, paisagem e uma seta de exportação, com verde menta e fundo escuro da interface. É uma identidade independente e não usa as marcas oficiais do Wallpaper Engine ou da Steam.

## Prompt final

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original Windows application logo and executable icon for Wallpaper Engine Scene Exporter, an independent utility that records animated wallpaper scenes and exports GIF or MP4. Generate one finished square icon, 1024x1024, not a presentation sheet. Design a distinctive, minimal geometric mark: two offset landscape frames suggesting successive animation frames, with an elegant export arrow integrated into the upper-right of the front frame. A simple rising landscape ridge within the frame evokes a wallpaper scene. Bold clean geometry and generous negative space, optically balanced, clear silhouette at 32px. Use mint green #8FE0B7 and near-white #F4F7F5 on a deep forest-black #0C1110 rounded-square tile. Flat vector-like graphic as a high-quality raster asset, crisp deliberate curves, consistent substantial strokes, no fine details, no shadows, no gradients, no 3D, no mockup, no words, no letters, no watermark. Tile fills approximately 88% of the square with transparent padding around it and genuinely transparent outer corners. Do not copy the Wallpaper Engine or Steam logos. Output only the single icon, centered, straight-on.
```
